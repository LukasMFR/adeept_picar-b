Controls
Joystick control
Use a drag joystick instead of the direction buttons. Great on a phone; the keyboard still works.
Speed follows finger distance
With the joystick on, how far you push sets the speed, so the speed slider is bypassed.
Robot behaviour
Invert forward / reverse
Swap the throttle direction if the robot drives the opposite way to the buttons.
Also invert the direction lights
Swap the white (forward) and red (reverse) LEDs to match.
Invert steering
Swap left and right if your steering is reversed.
Also invert the turn signals
Swap the left and right indicator LEDs to match.
Servo calibration

Fine-tune the resting center of each servo so the camera and steering sit straight. Values are stored on the robot and shared with every device, and the camera Center button uses them. Nudging a center gently moves that servo so you can see where straight really is.
